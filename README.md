# Vaylance

**Find jobs before they're posted.**

Vaylance is a job-search intelligence platform. It spots companies that are about to hire, matches them to what you're looking for, and helps you apply with a resume tailored to the role, all without breaking your formatting.

---

## What Vaylance Does

### Job Radar
Job Radar watches business news and public announcements for signs that a company is about to hire: new funding, expansions, new offices, contract wins and leadership changes.

- Tell Vaylance your target role, industry, country and work style before your first scan
- Get a feed of companies that match you, each with a fit score
- See **why now**, which roles are likely to open, and an estimated hiring window
- Every signal links back to its original source and date
- Remote roles are only shown when they look legitimate
- Write a ready-made outreach message, or add the company to your tracker in one click
- Mark signals as useful or not useful
- Works across many countries, including the US, UK, Canada, Australia, Ireland, India, Nigeria and South Africa, plus worldwide remote

### Resume + ATS
Check how well your resume fits a job description and improve it.

- Upload a PDF or Word (DOCX) resume, or paste the text
- Get one overall score that combines how well your experience fits the role, how your skills overlap, and which exact keywords hiring systems look for
- See your strengths, the keywords you're missing, and clear suggestions
- Let AI rewrite your Word resume for the job while keeping your original design
- Preview the result next to your score, then download it as Word or PDF
